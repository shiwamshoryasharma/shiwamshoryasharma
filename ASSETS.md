## Material and model detail

`surface-materials.js` generates shared stone, slate, timber, plaster and scale textures locally on canvas. Standard rough materials replace stepped toon lighting. Cottages include structural timber, recessed windows, shutters, door hardware, chimneys and roof trim. The articulated dragon uses tapered spline geometry, a curved neck and tail, horns, claws, scales and ribbed wing membranes. These are original procedural assets without additional model downloads.
