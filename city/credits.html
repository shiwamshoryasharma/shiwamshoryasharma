The explorer

The detailed humanoid base, face, hair, textures and rig are adapted from Sakurada Fumiriya by the VRoid Project / pixiv Inc. The publisher released this sample model under CC0. The conversion source is preserved in the project’s asset documentation.

The silver-blue palette, jade expedition mantle, collar, harness and navigation brooch were customized in Blender 5.2. Pixel, the floating robot companion, is original procedural Blender geometry. The explorer is a fictional avatar, not a likeness of Shiwam.

The world

The research pavilion, islands, vegetation, ruins, holograms, project exhibits, lighting and movement are original Blender and Three.js work. The world takes inspiration from anime adventure settings; it contains no ripped game characters, maps or soundtracks.

Music & sound

“Above the Workbench” is an original procedural ambient score written for this portfolio. Soft synthesized chords and sparse bell notes play with gentle interaction tones. All audio is synthesized locally with the Web Audio API. There are no third-party compositions, recordings, samples, audio downloads or music-service requests.

Sound starts muted. Use the music control to enable it and adjust the volume. Audio suspends while the tab is hidden.
